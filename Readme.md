# UK FMCG Consumer Trends Analysis (ONS)

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyses UK consumer spending trends in the FMCG sector using **ONS Consumer Trends data**, with a focus on:
- Real vs nominal consumption
- Inflation impact on food categories
- Volume-driven vs price-driven growth

The dashboard is built in **Power BI** and structured to support commercial, strategy, and category management decisions.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered
- Is FMCG growth driven by **real consumption (CVM)** or **price inflation (IDEF)**?
- Which food categories contribute most to volume growth?
- How has **YoY consumer demand** evolved post-cost-of-living pressures?
- Which COICOP levels explain the majority of spend?

---

## 📌 Key Insights
- Real food consumption continues to grow but at a **slowing rate (~2% YoY)**.
- Inflation accounts for an increasing share of nominal growth.
- Sugar and processed food categories show **disproportionate resilience**.
- Mid-level COICOP food categories drive most FMCG consumption.
