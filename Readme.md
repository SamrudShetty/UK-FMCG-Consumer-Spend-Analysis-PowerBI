# **UK FMCG Consumer Trends Analysis (ONS)**

📊 **Project Overview**  
This project explores UK consumer spending patterns within the **FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods)** sector using **ONS Consumer Trends** data. The analysis focuses on understanding how real consumption, inflation, and category-level dynamics shape overall market performance.

The Power BI dashboard is designed to support **commercial**, **strategy**, and **category management** teams by providing clear, actionable insights.

---

## 🎯 **Key Business Questions Answered**

- Is FMCG growth currently driven by **real consumption (CVM)** or **price inflation (IDEF)**?  
- Which food categories contribute most to **volume growth**?  
- How has **YoY consumer demand** evolved following cost-of-living pressures?  
- Which **COICOP levels** explain the majority of consumer spend?  

---

## 📌 **Key Insights**

- **Real food consumption is still growing**, but the pace has slowed to around **~2% YoY**.  
- **Inflation is driving a larger share of nominal growth**, widening the gap between CP and CVM.  
- **Sugar, sweets, and processed food categories** show **disproportionate resilience**, contributing more to volume change than their share of spend suggests.  
- **Mid-level COICOP categories** (Levels 3 and 4) account for the majority of FMCG spend, highlighting the importance of granular category analysis.

## 🧰 **Tools & Skills Demonstrated**

- **Power BI:** Data modelling, DAX measures, interactive visuals, narrative storytelling  
- **Economic Measures:** CP (Current Prices), CVM (Real Volume), IDEF (Implied Deflator)  
- **Time-Series Analysis:** YoY trends, inflation-adjusted comparisons  
- **FMCG Category Performance:** COICOP hierarchy analysis, volume vs price drivers  
- **Data Storytelling:** Translating economic data into commercial insights for non-technical stakeholders
